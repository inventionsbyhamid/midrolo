If you found sponsors through Podcorn, or Audacy Creator Lab as it has been called since 2025, time is short. Audacy
closes it on November 1, 2026, the sponsorship marketplace and the hosting service both.

Here's what to do before it closes, how to replace what it did for you, and where to look for sponsors now.

**The short version:**

- **Creator Lab closes on November 1, 2026.** The marketplace and the hosting both shut down, and it no longer takes
  new accounts.
- **Finish open campaigns and check your payout method.** Eligible earnings from completed campaigns are paid on the
  existing terms.
- **Save your records.** We found no export tool, so copy your earnings, your deals and the brands you worked with.
- **Remove the Podcorn analytics prefix** (pdcn.co) from your feed if you ever added it.
- **No single service does what Creator Lab did.** Most ad marketplaces need you to host with them or set a download
  minimum. Pitching brands yourself needs neither.

## What's closing, and when

Podcorn launched in 2019 as a marketplace for podcast sponsorships. Entercom, now Audacy, bought it in 2021, and in
2025 it added a hosting service and became Audacy Creator Lab.

In late August 2026, Audacy emailed users that Creator Lab, hosting and marketplace, would close on November 1, 2026.
Audacy told Inside Radio it was "a very small part of our overall podcasting business". The app now shows a notice,
"Creator Lab will be sunset on November 1, 2026", and sign up is closed to new accounts.

For podcasters, it was one of the few sponsorship marketplaces with no download minimum. Brands posted campaigns, you sent proposals at a price you set, and the brand paid up front. Creator Lab held the money until
the brand approved your ad, and charged a 10% fee to each side.

Most people affected used the marketplace, not the hosting: Podnews counted only about 426 shows hosted on Creator Lab
in the Podcast Index.

## Before November 1: a checklist

1. **Finish your open campaigns.** The shutdown notice, as quoted by PodGlue, says open campaigns have to be completed
   by November 1. Payment is released when the brand approves the ad, so nudge any brand that hasn't.
2. **Check your payout method.** Per the same notice, payment for eligible earnings from completed campaigns follows
   the existing payment terms. Creator Lab paid through Stripe or PayPal and held earnings until a payout method was
   set, so make sure yours is.
3. **Save your records.** We found no export tool. Screenshot or copy your Earnings page and your workrooms, where the
   briefs, talking points and dates live. For US taxes, Creator Lab's help center says payouts through Stripe or
   PayPal fall under the 1099-K rules, so keep those statements too.
4. **List the brands that sponsored you,** with the format, the price and how the read went. It's your sponsor history
   and your best lead list. Podcorn's terms barred dealing directly with brands met on the platform, so check the
   terms before you approach one directly.
5. **Remove the Podcorn analytics prefix.** If you ever connected your stats, your episode links may start with
   `https://pdcn.co/e/`. Creator Lab's notice asks sponsorship accounts to uninstall it, and Podnews warned on
   October 28 that shows still using it "will stop working on November 1". Open your RSS feed in a browser, search for
   `pdcn.co` anywhere in the episode links, and remove it in your host's analytics or prefix settings.
6. **If Creator Lab hosts your show, move it.** Pick a new host, move your episodes and set up a redirect so listeners
   and apps follow the show.

## After November 1: replace what the marketplace did

Creator Lab showed brands your numbers, brought brands to you and handled the money. Here's how to cover each job.

- **Your numbers.** Brands saw listens counted by the prefix. Now quote your host's numbers, as Podcorn's own FAQ
  told podcasters to do. Use downloads per episode in the first 30 days, not lifetime totals.
- **Your audience.** Creator Lab had no audience data of its own and suggested screenshots of age and gender from
  Spotify. Keep those, and add what your listeners love: the shows, brands and creators.
- **Your media kit and rates.** Put your numbers, audience, packages and prices on one page you can link in every
  pitch. Our guide to [what to charge for a podcast ad](/blog/podcast-sponsorship-rates) has a rate card to copy.
- **The money.** With no platform holding payment, agree the terms in writing before the first read and invoice the
  brand yourself. The same guide has a checklist of terms.

## Where to find sponsors now

The option with no minimum and no change of host is the one Creator Lab users already know: pitching brands at a price
you set. The difference is that you find the brands instead of waiting for campaigns. Our guide to
[finding sponsors for a small show](/blog/find-podcast-sponsors-small-show) shows how, step by step.

If you'd rather have brands come through a platform, here's what the main ones ask for, as of late October 2026:

| Service | Kind | What it needs | Pay and fees |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Libsyn Ads** | Host read marketplace, any host | 20,000 downloads an episode, on an IAB certified host | 70% to you; you approve each order |
| **Gumball** (Headgum) | Host read marketplace, any host | An application and review; reported minimums vary | You set your CPMs and dates; its fee isn't published |
| **SpotsNow** | Marketplace, any host | A free, verified listing; no stated minimum | You set prices; 10% fee only when a brand is new to your show |
| **Passionfroot** | Storefront and booking, any host | Free to join; aimed at B2B brands | You set your rates |
| **sponsorships.ai** | Listing, any host | Membership from $15 a month | No commission |
| **Acast Marketplace** | Your host's program | Acast hosting, and a paid plan for sponsorships | 50% of net revenue; Acast sets prices |
| **Podbean Ads Marketplace** | Your host's program | A paid Podbean plan, US only, 1,000+ monthly downloads | 70% of net; advertisers bid on CPM |
| **Spotify Partner Program** | Your host's program | Spotify for Creators hosting; in 30 days, 3 episodes, 2,000 hours and an audience of 1,000 | 50% of ad revenue; no pitching or bidding |
| **RedCircle Ad Platform** | A host's ad platform | About 500 weekly downloads on average | You set your CPM for host reads; 70% to you |

The pattern: host read marketplaces open to any host set high bars or vet you by application, and low minimums
usually come with hosting there, often for automatic ads rather than host reads. You can use more than one: list your
show, run your host's ads and pitch brands directly.

## How Midrolo works for podcasters

Midrolo is a podcast sponsorship marketplace that works the other way round from Creator Lab: brands search for shows
whose listeners fit them and send you a request, and you choose. All of this is free:

- **A page for your show, built from its RSS feed.** [Find your show](/podcasts); if it isn't listed yet, Midrolo adds
  it, with its episodes and the sponsor reads in recent show notes.
- **Claim it.** If your feed's owner email is the address you sign in with, the show is yours at once. Otherwise, use
  a 6-digit code sent to that email, or a short line in your show description. Creator Lab checked the email in your
  feed too, so this will feel familiar.
- **Your rates and open months** on your page, so brands see what a spot costs and when you have room.
- **Verified Spotify and Apple numbers, without a password.** Invite Midrolo's stats account to Spotify for Creators
  with Analytics access only, or to Apple Podcasts Connect with the Analyst role. Your numbers are labelled "Verified",
  with the service and the date.
- **Brand requests in one inbox.** No page shows your contact details. Brands request a spot, and you accept,
  decline, block or report.
- **No commission on any deal.** You agree the price with the brand and invoice them yourself, so there's no fee to
  build into your rates.
- **A free Snapshot,** if you also want to pitch: your top brand matches in a few minutes, ranked by how well they fit
  your listeners. Paid plans add AI sponsor reports and pitch packs; see [pricing](/pricing).

What it isn't: Midrolo doesn't hold payments, and it doesn't post campaigns for you to apply to. It's a listing brands
